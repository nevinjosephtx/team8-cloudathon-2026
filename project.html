Bank Marketing Prediction

Zach, Yousef, Ben, and Nevin

For this project, we used the UCI Bank Marketing dataset to predict whether a customer would subscribe to a term deposit. The goal was to help a bank decide whom to call using information available before the call.

We cleaned the data, compared classification models in Python, and selected a random forest based on validation F1 score.

Read the full project report

Results

On the test set of 5,911 customers, the model recommended 706 calls. Of those customers, 350 subscribed in the recorded data: a 49.6% hit rate, compared with 11.7% for the full test set.

This approach identified 50.8% of subscribers while calling 11.9% of the customer list. Calling fewer people also meant missing some potential subscribers.

The full report includes data cleaning, charts, model comparisons, and our recommendations. Results are from a held-out test set.
